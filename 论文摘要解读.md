# PIRBN 论文摘要解读（逐句）

## 说明

本文对 `文献.md` 的 Abstract 逐句解读。格式为“**摘要原句** + 解读”。并在文末总结“作者做了什么、收益是什么”。文中涉及符号统一采用 $$...$$ 形式，例如：$$\mathbf{u}_b \approx \alpha(\chi),(\mathbf{u}_{pres}-\mathbf{u}_f)+\mathbf{u}_{pres}$$。

---

## 一、摘要逐句解读

1. **Deep learning (DL) techniques have been extensively used to solve partial differential equations (PDEs) over the past decade [1].**  
   解读：先交代背景——过去十年，深度学习已经成为 PDE 数值求解的重要方向，说明问题有现实需求且已有技术积累。

2. **Among these, the physics-informed neural network (PINN) has earned increasing interest because it can be trained using both observation data (i.e. boundary conditions) and PDEs themselves [2].**  
   解读：指出 PINN 的核心优势：把数据约束和物理方程约束一起用于训练，本质是联合优化，例如 $$\mathcal{L}=\lambda_g\mathcal{L}_g+\lambda_b\mathcal{L}_b$$。

3. **By training neural networks with PDEs, PINN predictions show remarkable accuracy and excellent generalisation property [3].**  
   解读：强调 PINN 在许多任务中“精度高+泛化好”，这是本文继续研究 PINN 的前提。

4. **Now, PINN has been widely applied to various systems that are governed by PDEs, including mechanics modelling [4–15], material exploration [16–23], and medical studies [24–34], to name but a few.**  
   解读：说明 PINN 应用面广，覆盖力学、材料、医学，强调该方向的重要性与普适价值。

5. **Despite its empirical success, PINN suffers from difficulties in solving PDEs with high-frequency features or have ill-posed computational domains [2,35], and thus great efforts have been witnessed in improving PINN.**  
   解读：提出痛点：高频特征和病态区域（如平移后坐标域）下，PINN 训练困难，这是本文问题定义的起点。

6. **Choosing the appropriate physics laws to design effective physics-informed loss functions is one way to deal with the challenges [36].**  
   解读：第一类改进路线是“改损失函数”，即更好地设计物理约束项。

7. **Samaniego et al. [4] and Kharazmi et al. [37] formulated physics-informed loss functions for PINN training using the variational method.**  
   解读：举例说明已有工作通过变分法改造物理损失，提高训练效果。

8. **Haghighat et al. [38] and Yu et al. [39] embed not only PDEs but also gradient information obtained from sample points in the physics-informed loss function, rendering a significant enhancement in the performance of PINN for predicting gradient fields.**  
   解读：第二类细化是把梯度信息也加入损失，提升梯度场预测能力。

9. **Fuhg et al. [40] composed a mixed-form physics-informed loss function by integrating the variational formulation and PDEs.**  
   解读：继续说明“混合损失”思路：把变分式与 PDE 残差结合。

10. **Bai et al. [41] integrated the weighted residual method to weaken the constraints from PDEs.**  
    解读：通过加权残值法调节 PDE 约束强度，缓解训练不稳定。

11. **Apart from the above, decomposition and transformation with regard to the computational domain is another way to address the challenges.**  
    解读：第三类路线是“改计算域”，即分解域或坐标变换。

12. **Jagtap et al. [42] and Kharazmi et al. [37] divided computational domains into pieces.**  
    解读：把大域拆成子域，降低单模型拟合难度。

13. **In their work, the solution of PDEs in each subdomain was predicted by a corresponding PINN.**  
    解读：每个子域各训一个 PINN，再做拼接。

14. **Dong and Li [43] proposed the local extreme learning framework that can ensure the continuity conditions among the decomposed domains.**  
    解读：进一步解决“子域拼接连续性”问题。

15. **Based on the decomposition techniques, Shukla et al. [44] proposed a parallel computing technique to improve the training efficiency of PINNs.**  
    解读：分域方法还能天然并行，提升训练效率。

16. **Nguyen-Thanh et al. [45] applied the isoparametric technique to pre-normalise complex computational domains into simple geometries.**  
    解读：通过等参映射简化复杂几何域，降低学习难度。

17. **By using a distance-based encoding layer, Ramabathiran and Ramachandran [46] proposed sparse, physics-based, and partially interpretable neural networks (SPINN) that strengthen the interpretability of neural network for solving PDEs.**  
    解读：引入距离编码层，增强可解释性，也与本文“局部性”动机相关。

18. **The SPINN can be regarded as a generalised the meshless numerical method, while maintaining the powerful structure of DL models.**  
    解读：说明深度模型与无网格数值方法可融合，给本文后续 PIRBN 提供启发。

19. **Recent advances in terms of the theoretical study of DL, the so-called neural tangent kernel (NTK) theory, have also shed light to PINN.**  
    解读：转入理论线索：NTK 用来解释 PINN 训练动力学。

20. **The original NTK theory was proposed by Jacot et al. [47] and proved that training of infinite width neural networks with enough small learning rate can converge to Gaussian processes [48,49], which provided insights into DL training dynamics from the theoretical regard [50].**  
    解读：关键理论背景：无限宽 + 小学习率下，训练可由核方法近似，输出与高斯过程相关。

21. **The NTK theory was then introduced to PINN by Wang et al. [51].**  
    解读：说明 NTK 已被迁移到 PINN 场景。

22. **In their work, the training of PINN was analysed via the lens of NTK theory.**  
    解读：PINN 可用核矩阵谱来解释收敛快慢，例如模态衰减 $$e^{-\lambda_i t}$$。

23. **More importantly, an NTK-based adaptive learning scheme was proposed to balance the loss terms automatically.**  
    解读：给出工程价值：可自适应平衡多损失项，减少人工调参。

24. **With the help of the NTK-based adaptive learning scheme, different physics in PINNs can be equally trained and the PINNs can more effectively deal with problems that exhibit high-frequency features.**  
    解读：说明 NTK 不只是解释工具，也可指导算法改进，尤其改善高频问题。

25. **Later, Wang et al. [52,53] reported the connection between the NTK and the spectral bias in PINN, and tuned neural network structures to address multi-scale problems and long-term evolution problems.**  
    解读：进一步指出结构设计与谱偏置有关，这直接引出本文“重新设计网络结构”的思路。

26. **Li et al. [54] analysed the error estimation of PINNs through the NTK theory.**  
    解读：NTK 还用于误差估计。

27. **Xu et al. [55] studied the training dynamics of PINNs for inverse problems via the NTK theory and proposed a novel gradient descendent algorithm.**  
    解读：NTK 已覆盖正问题与逆问题，理论生态较完整。

28. **Inspired by the aforementioned studies, this work starts by studying the training dynamics of PINN via the NTK theory.**  
    解读：正式进入本文：先做 PINN 的 NTK 动力学分析，而不是直接提新模型。

29. **By visualising the evolution of a PINN’s NTK throughout training, we found that a PINN tends to be a local approximator after training.**  
    解读：核心发现：PINN 在训练后会趋向“局部逼近器”行为。

30. **Besides, when coping with PDEs that exhibit high-frequency features or have ill-posed computational domains, PINN fails for predictions due to the difficulties of training PINN into a local approximator.**  
    解读：进一步指出失败机理：难点在于“很难把 PINN 训成理想局部逼近器”。

31. **Thus, based on this finding, we tailored a neural network structure, namely the physics-informed radial basis network (PIRBN), that naturally possesses the local approximation property.**  
    解读：对应方案：提出 PIRBN，用径向基结构把局部性“内生化”，不是靠训练后才出现。

32. **The main contributions of this work are summarised as follows:**  
    解读：下面开始列贡献清单。

33. **(i) From numerical experiments and the NTK theory, we found that PINNs tend to become local approximators during training via gradient descendent algorithms.**  
    解读：贡献1：给出“PINN 局部化趋势”的实验与理论证据。

34. **(ii) Based on the above finding, we proposed the PIRBN, which has the local approximation property throughout the training.**  
    解读：贡献2：提出 PIRBN，并保证训练全过程都具备局部逼近属性。

35. **(iii) We prove that training the proposed PIRBN via gradient descendent algorithms also converges to Gaussian Processes when the width of the PIRBN tends to be infinite with an infinitesimal small learning rate.**  
    解读：贡献3：给出理论保证，PIRBN 在宽度极限下同样满足 NTK/GP 收敛框架。

36. **(iv) Comprehensive studies of the proposed PIRBN regarding parameter initialisation strategies, sizes of sample points and selections of radial basis activation functions are provided.**  
    解读：贡献4：给出完整工程配套，包括初始化、采样规模、核函数选择。

37. **(v) Challenging PDEs examples, which possess high-frequency features and ill-posed computational domains, are presented to show the performance of PIRBN with respect to PINN. Those problems that require domain decomposition techniques and multiple-layer PINNs can be now effectively solved by using single-layer PIRBNs.**  
    解读：贡献5：在高频与病态域任务上验证性能；原来要“分域+多层 PINN”的问题，可由单层 PIRBN 有效处理，复杂度更低。

38. **The paper is organised as follows: In Section 2, the basic conceptions of PINN and the NTK theory for PINN are briefly recalled. Meanwhile, by visualising the NTK of PINN through training processes, the local approximation property of PINN is observed. In Section 3, based on the finding, the PIRBN is proposed and formulated. Besides, the training dynamics of PIRBN are analysed by the NTK theory and the initialisation strategies of PIRBN are discussed in detail. In Section 4, several numerical examples, which are challenging for PINN, are conducted to show the better performance of PIRBN. In Section 5, the main conclusions of this work are summarised, and future perspectives are provided.**  
    解读：结构总览：第2节“发现问题与机理”、第3节“提出方法并给理论”、第4节“算例验证”、第5节“总结展望”，逻辑完整闭环。

## 二、作者干了什么（整篇论文视角）

1. 通过 NTK 视角分析 PINN 训练动力学，识别出“局部逼近器”这一关键行为。  
2. 针对高频与病态域痛点，提出具有天然局部性的 PIRBN 结构。  
3. 给出 PIRBN 在宽度极限下的理论收敛解释（与 GP/NTK 框架一致）。  
4. 提供可落地训练方案：参数初始化、采样规模、径向基函数选择。  
5. 用多个高难 PDE 任务做对比实验，证明 PIRBN 相比 PINN 更稳、更准。

---

## 三、有什么收益（方法价值）

- **收益1：训练稳定性提升**  
  局部结构先验降低了“必须靠训练才学出局部性”的难度。

- **收益2：高频/病态域能力更强**  
  在传统 PINN 易失败场景中，PIRBN 更易收敛并保持精度。

- **收益3：模型复杂度与工程成本下降**  
  一些原本依赖“分域+多网络”的任务可用单层 PIRBN 解决。

- **收益4：理论与工程统一**  
  不仅有实验效果，也有 NTK/GP 理论支持与参数化实践指南。
